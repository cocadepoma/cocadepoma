## Hi, I'm Paco 👋

**Frontend Tech Lead at [I-care](https://www.icareweb.com)** · Spain

I spent 13 years fixing industrial machines. Since 2019 I write the software that watches them.
Today I lead a team of 11 frontend engineers and I'm driving our move to **agentic development**:
architecture, review and technical judgement stay human — the typing doesn't.

### What I care about

- **Architecture before syntax.** Clean, Hexagonal and Screaming Architecture. The framework is a replaceable detail.
- **Fundamentals before frameworks.** Patterns, boundaries and bundlers outlive whatever is trending this year.
- **Tests that survive refactors.** Test from the user's side, not the implementation's.
- **AI is a tool.** The human leads, the model executes.
